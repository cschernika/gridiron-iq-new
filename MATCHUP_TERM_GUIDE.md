# Matchup Analyzer Term Guide

## Added to the Matchup Analyzer

- A clickable **ⓘ** information icon beside every matchup-table heading.
- A searchable **Matchup Term Guide** button at the top of the page.
- Plain-language explanations for all 34 RB, WR and TE matchup fields.
- Guidance explaining whether higher or lower values are favorable.
- Definitions for usage, coverage, likely defender, defensive back, secondary, offensive line, defensive front, run defense, scheme and composite matchup metrics.
- Existing click-to-sort behavior remains active on every sortable column.

## Included term groups

- Results
- Player and game information
- RB usage and efficiency
- Receiving usage
- Coverage and scheme fit
- Offensive-line and defensive-front trenches
- Run defense

The **Breakdown** definition also explains that the View button opens the player-specific matchup analysis.
