How the RB matchup score is calculated

40% Run Trenches

Current offensive-line run-blocking projection compared directly with the opponent’s run-defense grade.

25% RB Strength

Current production when available, otherwise the 2026 projection and position-relative skill rank.

20% Run Defense

Opponent rushing EPA, yards per carry, stuff rate and explosive-run rate.

15% Role & Receiving

Rush share, opportunity share, red-zone work, target share and RB receiving points allowed.

The preseason offensive-line projection uses the latest complete performance baseline and switches to current-season results through the weekly refresh.

How the WR/TE matchup score is calculated

30% Player Strength

Current production when available, otherwise preseason projection and position-relative skill rank.

40% Coverage Matchup

Opponent secondary, likely defender, positional points allowed and player man/zone splits.

18% Scheme Fit

How the player performed against the opponent’s dominant coverage tendency.

12% Pass Trenches

Offensive pass protection compared with opponent pass-rush strength.

“Likely defender” is a model estimate based on current position, snap share and coverage usage—not a guaranteed shadow assignment.
