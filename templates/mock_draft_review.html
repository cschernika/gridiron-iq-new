WHAT EACH SCORE MEANS

How Gridiron IQ graded your draft

ADP / Market Value 40%{{ grade.value_score }}/100

How efficiently you used draft capital. Value after ADP is rewarded, reaches are penalized progressively, and premium-round decisions count more.

Projected Point Potential 30%{{ grade.projection_score }}/100

Expected 2026 production relative to other players at the same position, so the grade rewards future scoring power without simply favoring quarterbacks.

Roster Need / Fit 20%{{ grade.need_score|default(0) }}/100

Rewards selections that fill missing starters and useful depth. Unnecessary positional duplication receives a lower score when more important needs remain.

Final Roster Construction 10%{{ grade.balance_score }}/100

Checks the completed roster for required starters, RB/WR depth, sensible positional counts and proper timing for kicker and D/ST.

Individual Pick Quality{{ grade.pick_quality_score }}/100

Supporting diagnostic: every pick is scored 45% ADP/value, 30% projected point potential and 25% roster need.

2025 Production · reference{{ grade.stats_score }}/100

Historical production remains visible as context, but it is no longer a direct percentage of the v6 overall draft grade.

RB/WR Depth{{ grade.depth_score }}/100

A supporting diagnostic for usable running back and wide receiver depth. It is not counted again as a separate percentage.
