DRAFT GRADE EXPLANATION

ⓘ How is my Draft Grade calculated?

Click to see the grading weights, definitions and grade scale.

The v6 score balances market value with what actually helps your fantasy team win. ADP still matters, but it can no longer overpower roster need or projected scoring potential.

Overall draft weighting: ADP / Market Value 40% · Projected Point Potential 30% · Roster Need / Fit 20% · Final Roster Construction 10%

ADP / Market Value — 40%

Measures whether you paid a fair market price. Picks after ADP earn value; reaches are penalized progressively. ADP is still the largest single factor, but it is no longer most of the grade.

Projected Point Potential — 30%

Rewards players expected to produce more fantasy points in 2026 relative to others at the same position. This keeps the grade focused on future scoring rather than reputation alone.

Roster Need / Fit — 20%

Rewards picks that fill missing starters or useful RB/WR depth. Drafting an unneeded extra QB, TE, kicker or defense is penalized, especially when more important roster needs remain.

Final Roster Construction — 10%

Checks the completed team as a whole: required starters, practical depth, sensible positional counts and proper timing for kicker and D/ST.

Individual Pick Score

Each pick is scored 45% ADP/value, 30% projected point potential and 25% roster need. That lets a strong high-upside player who fills an important need justify a modest reach.

2025 Production

Still appears in the draft review as useful context, but it is no longer a direct percentage of the overall grade. The new system emphasizes 2026 potential.

Depth Score

A supporting diagnostic showing how much usable RB/WR depth you created. It helps explain roster construction but is not counted again as a separate percentage.

Grade Scale

90–99 · EliteOutstanding value and roster construction with almost no costly mistakes.
85–89 · ExcellentClear draft advantage with only minor weaknesses.
80–84 · Very GoodStrong team with a few picks or roster spots that could be improved.
75–79 · SolidCompetitive draft with some opportunity cost or depth concerns.
70–74 · AveragePlayable roster, but several picks were inefficient or the roster is imbalanced.
65–69 · Below AverageToo many reaches, weak construction or insufficient impact production.
Below 65 · PoorMajor value losses or roster-building mistakes materially reduced draft quality.
