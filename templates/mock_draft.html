DRAFT GRADE EXPLAINED

What your Draft Grade actually means

The score is not just a projection total. It grades whether you selected the right players at the right prices and built a roster that makes sense for your league.

Completed draft weighting: Pick Quality 62% · Roster Construction 22% · 2026 Projection 9% · 2025 Proven Production 7%

Pick Quality — 62%

Grades every selection at the exact spot you made it. Earlier picks are weighted more heavily because mistakes with premium picks cost more. It combines ADP/market timing, projection strength and roster fit.

Roster Construction — 22%

Checks whether your team can fill required starters, has practical RB/WR depth, avoids excessive QB/TE drafting and does not spend premium picks on K or D/ST.

2026 Projected Production — 9%

Measures upcoming-season fantasy output relative to the player's own position so quarterbacks do not dominate the grade simply because they score more raw points.

2025 Proven Production — 7%

Uses actual 2025 fantasy production as evidence of an NFL track record. Rookies and players without meaningful 2025 history receive a neutral treatment rather than an automatic penalty.

Market Value / ADP

This is a major input inside Pick Quality. Drafting someone after his ADP is value. Drafting him well before ADP is a reach. The farther ahead of market you select a player, the larger the penalty.

Depth Score

A supporting diagnostic showing how much usable RB/WR depth you created. Depth is also reflected inside Roster Construction, so it is not counted again as a separate final-grade percentage.

How your live Draft Grade is built

Market Value / ADP—Supporting value signal showing whether your picks beat or trailed market cost. Large reaches are penalized more aggressively.
