HOW THE STRATEGY ENGINE USES THIS

Complete Roster + Best Available Talent

Gridiron IQ treats your required starters as hard end-of-draft requirements, but it does not force mediocre players too early. It compares quality and tier cliffs across positions, then increases need pressure as your remaining picks shrink.

Bench spots stay flexible. The engine uses scoring format and your selected strategy to build soft RB/WR/QB/TE depth targets, while still jumping on a clearly better player or a position group that is about to fall off a tier.

Example: if you still need TE and K with three picks left, those positions rise sharply. If you have six picks left and an elite WR falls, the engine can still take the value first because the roster can still be completed later.

DRAFT GRADE EXPLANATION

ⓘ How is my Draft Grade calculated?

Click to see the round-neutral grading formula and definitions.

v7 removes the automatic early-round weighting. A late-round pick is judged on the same 0–100 scale as an early pick. Strong late selections can raise your grade when they add quality, scoring upside, roster fit and a meaningful NFL role.

Overall: ADP 20% · Player Quality 20% · Scoring Potential 20% · Roster Need 15% · Usage / NFL Role 15% · Roster Construction 10%

ADP / Market Value — 20%

Rewards value and penalizes meaningful reaches, but ADP no longer dominates the score.

Player Quality — 20%

Blends scoring upside, current role/usage and proven production. It does not contain a round penalty.

Scoring Potential — 20%

Compares the player's 2026 projection with same-position players normally available around that draft range. A strong Round 12 option is graded against Round 12 alternatives, not against first-round stars.

Roster Need / Fit — 15%

Rewards filling open starters and useful depth while lowering unnecessary duplicate-position picks.

Usage / NFL Role — 15%

Uses current depth-chart role plus available snap, target and workload information. WR1/WR2, RB1/RB2, starting QB and starting TE roles receive more credit than reserve roles.

Final Roster Construction — 10%

Checks starters, practical RB/WR depth, position limits and sensible K/D/ST timing.

Individual Pick Score

Each pick is round-neutral: 20% ADP, 25% player quality, 25% scoring potential, 15% roster need and 15% usage/role.
