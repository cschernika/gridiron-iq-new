GRIDIRON IQ — PLAYER RESEARCH STRENGTH OF SCHEDULE
==================================================

SMALLEST UPDATE

1. Extract gridiron_iq_player_research_sos.zip.
2. Upload app.py to the ROOT of your GitHub repository and replace the existing app.py.
3. Upload templates/player_research.html to your repository's templates folder and replace the existing file.
4. Upload data/nfl_defensive_stats_2025.json to your repository's data folder and replace the existing file.
5. Preserve the folder names exactly.
6. Commit the changes to the main branch.
7. In Render, choose Manual Deploy > Deploy latest commit.
8. After deployment, hard-refresh the browser:
   Windows: Ctrl + F5
   Mac: Command + Shift + R

EXPECTED RESULT

- Player Research contains a sortable SOS column after Depth.
- SOS displays #1–#32 plus Easy, Average or Hard.
- Clicking a player opens a 2026 Strength of Schedule section.
- The profile lists all 18 weeks, the bye, opponent defensive rankings and weekly matchup ratings.
- Weeks 15–17 are highlighted and have a separate playoff SOS rank.

Rank #1 means the easiest fantasy schedule for that position.
