GRIDIRON IQ — PLAYER RESEARCH STRENGTH OF SCHEDULE
==================================================

SMALLEST UPDATE

1. Extract gridiron_iq_player_research_sos.zip.
2. Upload app.py to the ROOT of your GitHub repository and replace the existing app.py.
3. Upload templates/player_research.html to the templates folder and replace the existing file.
4. Commit both files to the main branch.
5. In Render, choose Manual Deploy > Deploy latest commit.
6. When deployment finishes, hard-refresh the browser:
   Windows: Ctrl + F5
   Mac: Command + Shift + R

EXPECTED RESULT

- Player Research has an SOS column after Depth.
- Clicking SOS sorts players by the position-adjusted schedule score.
- #1 represents the easiest schedule among 32 teams.
- Clicking a player opens a 2026 Strength of Schedule section.
- The profile shows the easiest weeks, toughest weeks and every weekly opponent.

Both app.py and templates/player_research.html are required. Do not upload app.py
inside the templates folder.
