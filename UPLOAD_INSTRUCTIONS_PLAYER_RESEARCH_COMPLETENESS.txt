PLAYER RESEARCH COMPLETENESS UPDATE

1. Download gridiron_iq_offensive_stats.zip.
2. Extract it on your computer.
3. Open your existing GitHub repository.
4. Upload the CONTENTS of the extracted gridiron_iq_offensive_stats folder to
   the root of the repository. Keep the templates and data folders as folders.
5. Choose "Commit changes" in GitHub.
6. Render should redeploy automatically. If it does not, choose Manual Deploy
   -> Deploy latest commit.

Do not create a second project folder inside the repository.
No new Render environment variable is required for this update.
