GRIDIRON IQ — SIDEBAR SYNC-ONLY FIX
====================================

OPTION 1 — SMALLEST UPDATE

1. Open the downloaded gridiron_iq_sidebar_sync_only_fix.zip file.
2. Upload app.py to the ROOT of your GitHub repository and replace the existing app.py.
3. Upload templates/dashboard.html to your repository's templates folder and replace the existing dashboard.html.
4. Commit the changes to the main branch.
5. In Render, open your Gridiron IQ service and choose Manual Deploy > Deploy latest commit.
6. After the deployment finishes, hard-refresh the browser:
   Windows: Ctrl + F5
   Mac: Command + Shift + R

OPTION 2 — COMPLETE PROJECT

Upload the contents of gridiron_iq_offensive_stats.zip to your repository,
preserving the included folders, then commit and redeploy.

EXPECTED RESULT

Under CONNECTIONS in the sidebar you should see only:
  League Sync

You should no longer see either:
  Connect League
  Connect a League

Do not put app.py inside the templates folder. Do not upload the outer ZIP file
directly into the repository; extract it first and upload its contents.
